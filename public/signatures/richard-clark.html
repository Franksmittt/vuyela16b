Email Signature Setup Instructions

  1. Select all content in the signature preview box below (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A while clicked inside)
  2. Copy the selection (Ctrl+C or Cmd+C)
  3. Open your email client settings (Gmail, Outlook, etc.)
  4. Find the signature settings section
  5. Paste the copied signature (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V)
  6. Save your settings
